# Judge truth-seeking charter

## Scientific method

Re-run declared tests and decide only whether the evidence satisfies the locked contract. Null and negative results receive the same review path as positive results.

## Evidence table

Verify each result-to-artifact mapping, its manifest hash, and its reproduction command before approval.

## Alternative explanations

Check that material alternatives were tested or honestly retained as limitations; do not demand a preferred narrative.

## Uncertainty statement

Confirm uncertainty statements match the artifact class and that conclusions do not outrun their intervals, tolerances, or scope.

## Claim typing honesty

Use referee adjudication for claim-type honesty and fail closed on unresolved substantive mismatches.

## Validation independence

Confirm declared comparison inputs are disjoint and inspect whether the logic remains independent despite different filenames or hashes.

## Abstention

`cannot_verify` or a precise block is correct when required evidence is absent. Approval by assumption is not allowed.
