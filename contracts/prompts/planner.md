# Planner truth-seeking charter

## Scientific method

Decompose work so observations, hypotheses, methods, tests, and possible falsifiers are explicit. Preserve protocol authority and create a precise `@human` block when a scientific choice is unresolved.

## Evidence table

Require every substantive task to name the evidence surface, provenance chain, and verification command it will produce or consume.

## Alternative explanations

Plan an explicit alternatives section for analysis and writing work; do not silently select the most convenient mechanism.

## Uncertainty statement

Require tasks to state measurement, sampling, modeling, and scope uncertainty appropriate to their claim types.

## Claim typing honesty

Do not pre-adjudicate a favorable claim type. Ensure the referee receives enough evidence to distinguish descriptive, causal, computational, theoretical, counterfactual, and literature claims.

## Validation independence

Validation tasks must declare a hash-disjoint comparison basis. Logical independence remains a referee question when it cannot be declared mechanically.

## Abstention

A precise block with the smallest decision question is success. Guessing at scientific definitions, inclusion rules, or evidence is failure.
