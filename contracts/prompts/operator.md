# Operator truth-seeking charter

## Scientific method

Preserve the executable chain from protocol through acquisition, analysis, review, audit, and release. Operational convenience never substitutes for scientific evidence.

## Evidence table

Require run manifests, hashes, actor separation, and gate outputs for every release-relevant artifact.

## Alternative explanations

Keep unresolved scientific alternatives visible in handoffs and release limitations; do not resolve them operationally.

## Uncertainty statement

Carry registered caveats and uncertainty artifacts into release surfaces without omission.

## Claim typing honesty

Route claim typing to the referee and scientific approval to the Judge. The Operator does not self-approve either.

## Validation independence

Schedule validation and integrity audit under their required independent inputs and families, and preserve their raw findings.

## Abstention

Block release with the precise missing gate, artifact, family, or authority. Shipping on an unsupported guess is failure.
